The problem

Downtime is the silent killer of drone economics.

Every industrial fleet hits the same wall: ~25 minutes of flight, then ~40 minutes on a charger. On a site where an unplanned failure costs tens of thousands of dollars per hour, a grounded drone is a stalled inspection or a part that never arrives.

02 · Ground Control

The fleet routes itself around energy.

Ground Control watches every battery in real time. When a pack runs low, the system weighs distance, station queue depth, and charged-pack availability across the network — then redirects the aircraft automatically. Operators keep full manual override. Watch DR-07's battery drain: the reroute fires on its own.

Privacy Policy

Last updated · July 2026

Reswap ("we," "us," or "our") builds battery-swap infrastructure and fleet-management software for industrial drone operations. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it — both on this website and inside the Reswap platform.

Information we collect

Website. When you contact us through this site, we collect the information you provide: your name, work email, company, and message. We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate site traffic; we do not build individual visitor profiles.

Platform. When your organization uses the Reswap platform, we process operational data required to run the service:

  • Account information — operator names, work emails, and roles within your organization
  • Fleet telemetry — drone position, battery state, and flight status during operations
  • Swap records — timestamps, drone and pack identifiers, cycle counts, and temperatures logged at each battery swap
  • Mission data — mission plans, progress, and completion records created by your operators

How we use it

  • To operate the service: routing drones to stations, managing swaps, and running your missions
  • To maintain safety: monitoring battery health and flagging packs for replacement
  • To improve battery performance models: swap records are aggregated and de-identified before analysis
  • To respond when you contact us

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your data for advertising.

Customer data isolation

Each customer's fleet, station, and mission data is logically isolated within the platform. Operators can only access data belonging to their own organization. Aggregated battery-performance statistics used to improve our models are stripped of customer and site identifiers.

Data retention

Operational records are retained for the duration of your service agreement plus any period required for safety auditing and regulatory compliance. Contact-form submissions are retained for up to 24 months. You may request deletion of your personal information at any time, subject to legal retention requirements.

Security

Platform access is authenticated and role-based. Data is encrypted in transit. We follow industry-standard practices for credential storage and access control, and we review these practices as the platform evolves.

Third parties

We use a small number of infrastructure providers (hosting, email) to run our service. These providers process data on our behalf under contractual confidentiality obligations. We do not share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@reswap.io.

Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above. Material changes affecting platform customers will be communicated directly.

Contact

Questions about this policy or our data practices: privacy@reswap.io.
